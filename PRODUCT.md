# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, equal weight:

- Local property owners in Lefkada who need a building permit, a renovation, a
  legalisation of unauthorised works, an energy upgrade ("Εξοικονομώ"), or a
  topographic survey.
- Buyers from elsewhere (Athens, abroad) who own or buy a plot on the island and
  want a holiday house built while they live far away.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for the technical office of Αλέξανδρος Βλάχος, civil engineer in
Lefkada. Success: the visitor calls, emails, or books a site visit.

## Positioning

One office can take the whole project, from survey and permit to construction,
and the client talks to the same engineer throughout. Architects are part of the
team. First site visit in Lefkada is free.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no build: HTML, CSS, JS, three.js from CDN. Live at
  axonas.haroldpoi.dev.
- Content lives in `data/site.js` (services, steps, team) and
  `data/projects.js` (projects, categories as filters).
- Contact form does not send yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Αλέξανδρος Βλάχος, Πολιτικός μηχανικός, Τεχνικό γραφείο.
- Logo: `logo.svg`, two polylines (an A and a V) drawn as line work.
- The client prefers a simple, black-and-white style that looks good with the
  logo. Style, effects, and animation matter more than copy.
- Greek is the site language.
